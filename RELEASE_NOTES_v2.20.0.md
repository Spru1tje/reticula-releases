# Reticula 2.20.0

**Release date:** 2026-11-22

## Downloads

- Windows 10/11: `Reticula_Setup_2.20.0.exe` (not code-signed - SmartScreen: *More info* → *Run anyway*)
- macOS, Apple Silicon: `Reticula_2.20.0_macOS_arm64.dmg` (not notarised - first start: right-click → *Open*)
- `SHA256SUMS.txt` to verify the downloads.

## What is new

- **Reticon: about 5000 vector icons** (was about 1400). New families cover glassware, instruments, devices, materials,
  spectra, plates, pipes, reactors, crystals, gas cylinders, process flow, charts, racks, rotors and cuvettes.
- **Dark mode by default** (white stays available in the sidebar).
- **CIE 1931 colour diagram** painted in its colours (PL module).

## Known limits

- The new icons do not yet reach a BioRender-level finish: no shading or depth.
- Chart colours are not all checked one by one on dark backgrounds.

Full list: `app/CHANGELOG.md`.
